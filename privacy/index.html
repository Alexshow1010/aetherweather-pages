AetherWeather 隱私政策

本政策依 2026-10-28 的 App 功能、Google Mobile Ads SDK 及 User Messaging Platform 整合情況編寫；不宣稱已經獨立法律審查。

App 目的

AetherWeather 顯示使用者選擇城市的氣象資料，並把氣象數值轉換為非占卜性的環境節律介面。

Apple 氣象與地理搜尋服務

當您搜尋城市時，搜尋文字會交由 Apple MapKit 處理。選擇城市後，該城市的座標會交由 Apple WeatherKit 取得氣象資料。這些 Apple 服務的處理受 Apple 適用條款與隱私政策約束。

定位權限

本版本不要求 GPS 定位權限、不要求「永遠允許」位置權限，也不執行背景定位。城市位置由您主動搜尋及選擇。

本機儲存與保存期間

已儲存城市、座標、時區、目前選擇及顯示語言偏好會透過 iOS UserDefaults 保存在裝置上，直到您變更或移除設定、刪除 App，或清除 App 資料。開發者沒有自有伺服器保存此資料。

廣告與 Google SDK

AetherWeather 的廣告版本可能在首頁顯示 Google Mobile Ads 提供的橫幅廣告，並使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）管理適用的同意與隱私選擇。廣告無法載入、使用者拒絕同意或退出資料銷售或分享時，天氣、預報、城市管理與氣象卦流仍可使用。

可能由 Google 處理的資料

依裝置、地區、同意狀態、Apple 系統權限與廣告設定，Google Mobile Ads 與 UMP 可能處理由 IP 位址推估的概略位置、裝置或廣告識別碼、廣告資料、App 內互動、當機、效能及其他診斷資料。用途可能包含第三方廣告、開發者廣告、分析、防止欺詐及服務運作。實際處理取決於 Google 服務設定、地區法規與您的選擇。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策 與 Google 廣告技術說明。

地區同意與隱私選擇

App 每次啟動會透過 UMP 更新同意資訊。若所在地區需要同意或退出選擇，App 會顯示 Google 提供的隱私訊息。您可在 App「設定」中重新開啟適用的隱私選項。若同意資訊暫時無法取得，App 不會因此中斷核心功能，並只會在 UMP 回報可請求廣告後載入廣告。

App Tracking Transparency

若廣告設定涉及 Apple 所定義的跨 App 或網站追蹤，AetherWeather 會使用 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）系統提示取得授權。UMP 同意不等於 ATT 授權；未授權時不應使用 IDFA 進行追蹤。拒絕 ATT 不會停用核心功能。您可於 iOS「設定」變更追蹤權限。

帳號與公開網頁

App 沒有帳號系統，開發者不營運儲存城市或氣象資料的自有伺服器。本公開支援與隱私頁不使用 Cookie 或分析程式。

使用者選擇

您可移除已儲存城市、刪除 App 以移除本機偏好、在 App 設定管理適用的 Google 隱私選擇，並在 iOS 設定管理 ATT 權限。

AetherWeather Privacy Policy

This policy reflects the app functionality and its Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform integration as of October 28, 2026. It does not claim independent legal review.

Purpose of the App

AetherWeather displays weather for cities selected by the user and translates weather values into a non-divinatory environmental rhythm interface.

Apple Weather and Place Search

When you search for a city, the search text is processed by Apple MapKit. After you choose a city, its coordinates are provided to Apple WeatherKit to request weather data. Apple's processing is governed by its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Location Permission

This version does not request GPS access, Always Location permission, or background location. You manually search for and select a city.

On-Device Storage and Retention

Saved cities, coordinates, time zones, the selected city, and your display-language preference are kept on your device using iOS UserDefaults until you change or remove them, delete the app, or clear its data. The developer does not operate a server that stores this information.

Advertising and Google SDKs

An advertising-enabled AetherWeather build may display a banner supplied by Google Mobile Ads on the Home screen and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Weather, forecasts, city management, and Weather Flow remain available if an ad cannot load, consent is declined, or a user opts out of the sale or sharing of data.

Data Google May Process

Depending on the device, region, consent status, Apple system permissions, and advertising configuration, Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process general location inferred from an IP address, device or advertising identifiers, advertising data, in-app product interaction, crashes, performance data, and other diagnostics. Possible purposes include third-party advertising, developer advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Actual processing depends on Google's service configuration, regional law, and your choices. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies information.

Regional Consent and Privacy Choices

The app updates consent information through UMP on every launch. If consent or an opt-out choice is required in your region, the app displays the privacy message supplied by Google. Applicable choices can be reopened from AetherWeather Settings. If consent information is temporarily unavailable, core features remain available, and the app loads ads only after UMP reports that ads can be requested.

App Tracking Transparency

If the advertising configuration involves tracking across apps or websites as defined by Apple, AetherWeather uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system prompt to request authorization. UMP consent is not ATT authorization, and IDFA should not be used for tracking without authorization. Denying ATT does not disable core features. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings.

Accounts and Public Pages

The app has no account system, and the developer does not operate a server that stores saved cities or weather data. These public support and privacy pages use no cookies or analytics.

Your Choices

You may remove saved cities, delete the app to remove local preferences, manage applicable Google privacy choices in AetherWeather Settings, and manage ATT permission in iOS Settings.
