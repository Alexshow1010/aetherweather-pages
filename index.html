關於 AetherWeather

AetherWeather 將 Apple Weather 氣象資料與六十四卦符號結合，呈現城市天氣的環境節律。卦象由氣象數值確定性映射，用於閱讀天氣變化，不是算命或命運預測。

這是一款原生 iOS App，提供即時氣象、未來 12 小時趨勢、七日預報、城市搜尋與繁體中文／英文介面。

About AetherWeather

AetherWeather combines Apple Weather data with a 64-hexagram visual language to express a city's environmental rhythm. Hexagrams are mapped deterministically from weather values. They are not fortune-telling or predictions about fate.

The native iOS app provides current conditions, a 12-hour outlook, a seven-day forecast, city search, and Traditional Chinese and English interfaces.
