使用說明

AetherWeather 顯示 Apple Weather 氣象資訊，並以六十四卦作為天氣節律的視覺語言。

切換顯示語言

前往「設定」的「顯示語言」，可選擇「跟隨系統」、「繁體中文」或「English」。變更會立即套用並保留至下次開啟 App。

如何閱讀 Weather Flow

首頁提供即時氣象、未來 12 小時趨勢與當日卦流。上卦呈現較廣的氣象趨勢，下卦呈現較近的當下體感。這些內容是環境閱讀提示，不是預言。

搜尋與切換城市

  1. 開啟「城市」。
  2. 輸入城市名稱並等待搜尋結果。
  3. 點選候選城市以新增並切換。
  4. 點選已儲存城市即可再次切換；由管理動作可移除城市。

WeatherKit 資料來源

氣象資料由 Apple Weather 提供。App 內會在連線成功後顯示 Apple Weather 標示與法律資訊連結。官方警報永遠優先於節律提示。

常見問題

資料無法載入：確認網路連線，稍後重試，並檢查城市是否正確。Apple Weather 或 MapKit 暫時無法使用時，App 會保留已儲存城市而不應崩潰。

搜尋不到城市：嘗試當地名稱、英文名稱或較完整的行政區名稱。

安全與非命理聲明

卦象由天氣數值確定性產生，不提供算命、財運、感情或命運預測。生活節律內容不取代醫療建議、安全指示或官方氣象警報。

Using the App

AetherWeather presents Apple Weather information and uses the 64 hexagrams as a visual language for weather rhythm.

Change the Display Language

Open Settings and choose Language, then select System Default, Traditional Chinese, or English. The change takes effect immediately and remains selected after you reopen the app.

How to Read Weather Flow

The Home screen shows current weather, the next 12 hours, and today's Weather Flow. The upper trigram describes the broader trend; the lower trigram describes nearby conditions and current feel. These are environmental prompts, not predictions.

Search and Switch Cities

  1. Open Cities.
  2. Enter a city name and wait for results.
  3. Select a candidate to save and switch to it.
  4. Select any saved city to switch again, or use the management action to remove it.

WeatherKit Data Source

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Once connected, the app displays Apple Weather attribution and a link to legal details. Official weather alerts always take priority over rhythm prompts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Weather will not load: Check your connection, try again later, and confirm the selected city. If Apple Weather or MapKit is temporarily unavailable, the app should retain saved cities without crashing.

No city result: Try the local name, English name, or a more complete administrative-area name.

Safety and Non-Divination Statement

Hexagrams are generated deterministically from weather values. The app does not provide fortune-telling or predictions about finances, relationships, or fate. Rhythm notes do not replace medical advice, safety instructions, or official weather alerts.
